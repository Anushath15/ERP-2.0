setRemNote | The program never sends WhatsApp messages by itself. It opens WhatsApp with your message ready, and you press Send. Each opening is counted as a reminder.
setRemTone | Message style
setRemTemplate | Message text
setRemUse | You can use these words in the text. They are filled in for each customer:
setRemReset | Use the standard text
setShopPhone | Shop phone (shown in the message)
setUpi | UPI ID (shown in the message)
setGuard | Ask before reminding again within (hours)
setCountry | Default country code
setCreditTitle | Credit
setLimitMode | When a bill goes over the credit limit
limitOff | Do nothing
limitWarn | Warn me
limitBlock | Do not allow
remsTooltipOpen | Opens WhatsApp with this message ready. You press Send there.
